---
title: Human approval
description: Pause before risky tool calls, decide from anywhere, and record who decided.
---


Name the tools a person must approve. When the model calls one, the run
pauses **before any tool of that turn runs** and returns the requests. The
pause is saved with the thread, so the decision can come minutes or days
later, from another process, a dashboard or a webhook.

<Sample name="approvals" />

## Decisions

| Decision | What happens |
|---|---|
| **approve** | The call runs as the model wrote it |
| **edit** | The call runs with your arguments (or another tool); the model is told what actually ran |
| **reject** | The call does not run; the model sees your reason |
| **respond** | The call does not run; your answer is the tool's result |

`approve()` and `reject(reason)` answer every pending call; `decide` takes
one decision per call id. A decision that does not fit (an unknown call id,
arguments that fail the schema) is refused with `resume_invalid` and the
thread keeps waiting.

## While it waits

- Sending a new message fails with `thread_suspended` until the approval is
  answered.
- `reply.approvals` lists what waits: call id, tool, arguments.
- With the [audit trail](/docs/guides/compliance/) on, the request, the
  decision and who made it (`actor`) are recorded, and the decision is
  recorded **before** the call runs.
